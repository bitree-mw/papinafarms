history_edu Origin & Trajectory

Solving the "Missing Middle" in Malawi's Agro-Economy

In 2020, Papina Farms Ltd was incorporated in Mzuzu to resolve an endemic structural failure across Northern Malawi: despite high farmer productivity, over 30% of harvested grain and oilseeds decayed prior to reaching consumer markets due to sub-standard storage, distress selling, and non-existent local primary processing.

By investing in decentralized rural collection points, certified electronic weighbridges, and bulk hermetic silo storage in Mzuzu, we turned subsistence farmers into bankable commercial outgrowers.

Today, Papina Farms stands as an integrated agribusiness enterprise: aggregating staple grains, sunflower, soybeans, and pulses; operating processing lines; and supplying structured institutional buyers, regional grain reserves, and consumer food brands.
