## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, forward-looking African agribusiness enterprise rooted in Mzuzu, Malawi. The aesthetic bridges institutional credibility, commercial vitality, and organic agricultural stewardship. It communicates stability, scale, transparency, and sustainable cultivation to international partners, regional stakeholders, off-takers, and financial institutions.

The visual style blends **Corporate / Modern** precision with **Tactile Organic Warmth**:
- Generous, clean fields of crisp warm cream and white establish clarity and institutional governance.
- Rich forest and agricultural greens anchor the operational reality of commercial farming, agronomy, and environmental resilience.
- Vibrant golden maize yellows and deep alluvial browns bring focal vitality, sunlit optimism, and harvest value.
- Rounded surfaces, micro-textured borders, and delicate, high-definition data displays convey corporate maturity without sterility.

## Colors

The palette strictly implements a 60-30-10 distribution rule to ensure institutional authority and legibility:

### 60% Canvas & Spatial Grounds
- **Pure Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` — Primary interior backgrounds for cards, modal surfaces, data tables, and high-density operational panels.
- **Warm Cream Ground:** `#FBF8F0` — Canvas underlay, global viewport background, alternating section backdrops, and atmospheric groupings.

### 30% Structural Green & Identity
- **Primary Forest Green:** `#075C38` — Brand headers, primary action buttons, enterprise navigation bars, high-level KPIs, and executive typography.
- **Secondary Agricultural Green:** `#387A43` — Supporting charts, active tab states, sub-navigation, progress indicators, and operational status accents.
- **Surface Tint Greens:** `#E7F1EB` (Subtle 8% wash of primary) used for chip backgrounds, table hover states, and input field focuses.

### 10% Accents & Value Focus
- **Golden Harvest Yellow:** `#F5B719` — Alert tags, conversion CTAs, interactive highlights, harvest metrics, and key performance milestones.
- **Warm Earthy Brown:** `#87521E` — Soil, provenance badges, secondary metadata chips, geographical indicators, and financial yield labels.

### Typography & Neutrals
- **Dark Charcoal Neutral:** `#252B26` — High-contrast primary reading text, form labels, and table typography.
- **Muted Silt:** `#5C665E` — Secondary explanatory copy, helper text, and inactive icon states.
- **Border Stroke:** `#E5E0D5` — Soft, warm baseline outlines for cards and input boundaries.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, humanistic display headers with **Inter** for dense, clean corporate operations and data parsing.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides structural dignity with warm, rounded terminals, echoing modern African enterprise confidence. Use it for page banners, section leads, hero value propositions, and summary scorecards.
- **Inter** ensures uninterrupted data transmission across supply chain tables, crop schedules, logistics readouts, and legal notices. Its tall x-height maintains maximum readability across low-bandwidth field devices and multi-column desktop monitors.
- Numerals in analytical tables must adopt tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve column vertical alignment across metric counters and currency summaries (MWK / USD).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a disciplined 8pt grid with a responsive, container-bound fluid grid structure:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Max layout frame caps at 1440px to ensure institutional dashboards maintain unified spatial integrity.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Multi-metric enterprise rows reflow into balanced 2x2 cards.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) edge margins. Complex agricultural tables collapse into stacked cards with horizontal scroll drawers for row metrics.

Outer layout sections employ generous breathing room (`space-xl` to 64px) between distinct business units (e.g., Macadamia processing, livestock, irrigation summaries), reinforcing confidence, scale, and clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient warm shadows** coupled with **tonal surface stacking** rather than aggressive artificial elevations:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FBF8F0` canvas tint with transparent borders.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FFFFFF` fill bounded by a 1px solid stroke in `#E5E0D5`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(37, 43, 38, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(7, 92, 56, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** `#FFFFFF` fill with elevated depth: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(37, 43, 38, 0.07), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(7, 92, 56, 0.04)`. Border shifts subtly to `#387A43` at 30% opacity.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a grounded ambient envelope: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(37, 43, 38, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(7, 92, 56, 0.06)`. Accompanied by a 40% opacity backdrop overlay (`rgba(37, 43, 38, 0.45)`) featuring a subtle 4px backdrop blur.

Shadows are explicitly tinted with trace green and warm charcoal pigments, eliminating sterile synthetic greys in favor of rich, fertile atmospheric depth.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape model (`level 2`) to balance corporate institutional rigor with organic agricultural contours:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Chips):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius. This creates clean, friendly touchpoints while retaining structural alignment.
- **Content Cards, Panels, & Modal Dialogue Boxes:** `1rem` (16px) radius (`rounded-lg`). Ensures large white surfaces feel approachable and crafted.
- **Featured Banners & Hero Presentation Enclosures:** `1.5rem` (24px) radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pill Badges & Circular Indicators:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) reserved specifically for crop status pills, operational tags, and user avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Corporate CTA:** Solid `#075C38` background, white text (`Inter SemiBold`), `0.5rem` radius, `0 2px 4px rgba(7, 92, 56, 0.2)` shadow. Hover shifts to `#387A43` with an elevation increase to Level 2.
- **Accent Harvest CTA:** Solid `#F5B719` background, `#252B26` text. Used sparingly for high-value actions (e.g., "Invest", "Order Shipment", "Download Audit").
- **Secondary / Outline:** 1.5px border in `#075C38`, transparent background, `#075C38` text. Hover fills with 8% green tint (`#E7F1EB`).
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#075C38` or `#5C665E` text for secondary utility actions.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` surfaces, `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) border radius, and 1px stroke in `#E5E0D5`.
- Header areas feature clean vertical separation using a bottom border of 1px in `#F5F2EA`.
- Accent cards (such as agronomy bulletins or key harvest metrics) may feature a 4px left-border accent in `#075C38` or `#F5B719`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Operational / Normal:** `#E7F1EB` background, `#075C38` text, pill shape (`9999px`), padding `4px 12px`.
- **Harvest / Yield:** `#FEF8E8` background, `#87521E` or darkened `#F5B719` text.
- **Provenance / Soil Region:** `#F4ECE4` background, `#87521E` text with subtle geographic pinpoint icon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`; border: 1.5px solid `#E5E0D5`; border-radius: `0.5rem`; padding: `10px 14px`. Text in `#252B26`, placeholder in `#8A948C`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions cleanly to `#075C38` with an outer ring glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(7, 92, 56, 0.15)`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Unchecked: 1.5px border in `#8A948C` with white interior.
- Checked: Solid `#075C38` fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or radio dot. Radius: Checkbox uses `4px`; Radio uses `9999px`.

### Lists & Enterprise Data Tables
- Header row uses `#FBF8F0` with subtle upper-case labels (`label-sm`) in `#5C665E`.
- Rows feature alternating white and soft neutral tints, with 1px border-bottom in `#E5E0D5`. Hover state triggers `#E7F1EB` background fade.
- Metric cells display numbers in tabular Inter numerals for immediate vertical scanning.

### Specialized Agribusiness Components
- **Crop Yield & Soil Metric Meters:** Dual-tone progress trackers using `#075C38` (attained) against `#E7F1EB` (target ceiling).
- **Outgrower & Commodity Badges:** Institutional credential tags displaying Mzuzu origin seals, fair trade compliance markers, and moisture content data points with dark `#87521E` grounding.
