About Papina Farms Ltd

Rooted in Northern Malawi, Growing Inclusive Agribusiness

Founded in 2020 in Mzuzu, Papina Farms Ltd emerged with an uncompromising purpose: transforming subsistence farming across Malawi into profitable, sustainable, and commercially disciplined enterprises.

Operating from the agro-ecological cradle of Mzuzu and the Northern Region, we bridge systemic bottlenecks by combining community mobilization, modern post-harvest storage, precision agronomy, and assured off-taking contracts. Our integrated agribusiness model safeguards the farmer's livelihood while delivering certified produce to regional food security channels.
